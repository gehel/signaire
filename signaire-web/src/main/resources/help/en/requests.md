# Requesting a sign

Is a sign missing? Ask the administrators: they look for it on SignSuisse and add it if they find it.

- In **My requests**, give the word or expression, the sign language and, if you want, a comment (meaning,
  context).
- When a search finds nothing, the "Request" link fills in the word for you.

You need an [account](/help/account).

## Following your requests

**My requests** lists your requests and where they are:

- **Waiting**: the administrators haven't handled it yet;
- **Added**: the sign is in the lexicon, a link leads to it;
- **Rejected**: the sign could not be added.
