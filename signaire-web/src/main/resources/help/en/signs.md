# The page of a sign

The page of a sign shows everything SignSuisse says about it:

- the **video** of the sign, which plays once: the controls of the player play it again or pause it, and the
  **Slow motion** button plays it again once at half speed, after which it plays at the normal speed again;
- the **images** (photo or drawing);
- the **periphrase** and the **definition**;
- the **categories**, which lead to the other signs of the category;
- the **dialects** where the sign is used and its **regional variants** (mostly in DSGS);
- **examples**, sometimes with their video;
- a link to the page of the sign on **SignSuisse**.

## Three sign languages

Switzerland has three sign languages: French sign language (LSF), Swiss German sign language (DSGS) and Italian sign
language (LIS). The same word has a different sign in each, each with its own page.

## The data

The signs, their videos and images come from the [SignSuisse](https://www.sgb-fss.ch/signsuisse/fr) lexicon of the
Swiss Federation of the Deaf SGB-FSS, which remains their owner.
