# Your account

You don't need an account to search and look at signs. You need one for [lists](/help/lists) and
[requests](/help/requests).

## Creating an account

Choose "Log in" then "Create an account":

- the **username** has 3 to 40 characters: unaccented letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores; capitals don't
  matter;
- the **name shown** is optional: it is the name Signaire shows;
- the **password** has at least 8 characters.

Depending on the configuration, creating accounts may be closed, or logging in with another account (OpenID Connect)
possible: a "Log in with…" button then appears. Your Signaire account is created at the first login.

## Logging out

"Log out" is in the header of every page.
