# Exporting

A list or the results of a search can be downloaded in three formats. Open "Export" under the list or the results,
choose the format, then "Download".

## Printable PDF

Each sign with its name, language, categories, image, definition and a **QR code**: scan it with a phone to watch
the video of the sign.

| Layout | What for | How to print |
|---|---|---|
| Sheets | A grid of signs per page (1 to 12) | Normally |
| Booklet to fold | A small booklet | Double-sided, flipping on the **short edge**; stack the sheets and fold them in the middle |
| Double-sided flashcards | Cards to cut: the image on one side, the word on the other | Double-sided, flipping on the **long edge**; cut along the lines |

The paper can be A4, A5 or A6. For the booklet it is the size of the sheets: a booklet on A4 has A5 pages.

## Anki deck

[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) is free flashcard software for computers, Android (AnkiDroid) and iPhone
(AnkiMobile). It shows each card when you are about to forget it (spaced repetition).

The deck has two cards per sign: **sign → word** (you see the video, you find the word) and **word → sign**.
Downloaded videos and images are included. Import the `.apkg` file in Anki with File › Import. Importing a deck again
updates its cards without losing your reviews.

## E-book (EPUB)

For e-readers and reading apps: a title page, a table of contents and a page per sign. Videos are links; check
"Include the videos" to put them in the book (few e-readers play them, and the book gets big).

The texts of the documents are in the language of the interface. A search exports 500 signs at most.
