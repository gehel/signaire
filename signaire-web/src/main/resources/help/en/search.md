# Searching signs

Type a word in the search field: the results appear as you type.

- Signaire searches the **name** of the sign, its **periphrase**, its **definition** and its **examples**.
- Accents and capitals don't matter: `eveque` finds ÉVÊQUE.
- Inflected forms are recognized: `chats` finds CHAT.
- The beginning of a word is enough: `laus` finds LAUSANNE.

Without words, Signaire lists all the signs in alphabetical order.

## Filtering

- **Sign languages**: LSF (French-speaking Switzerland), DSGS (German-speaking Switzerland) or LIS (Ticino). With
  none checked, all three are searched.
- **Category**: the categories of SignSuisse (City, Animals, Religion…).

Results come in pages of twenty. Each result leads to the [page of the sign](/help/signs) and to its page on
SignSuisse.

## Is the sign missing?

When the search finds nothing, a link offers to [request the sign](/help/requests) from the administrators.

Once logged in, you can also add results to your [lists](/help/lists) and [export](/help/exports) them.
